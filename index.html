Roofing

Your Roof.
Our Fight.

When hail or wind damages your roof, your insurance company wants to pay as little as possible. Forge Restoration has navigated hundreds of claims — we document every shingle, every vent, every flashing point so your adjuster sees the full picture. Most homeowners pay nothing out of pocket beyond their deductible.

  • Full storm damage inspection — completely free
  • Insurance adjuster walk-through & advocacy
  • GAF & Owens Corning certified materials
  • Lifetime workmanship warranty
  • Tear-off & full replacement
  • Emergency tarping & leak repairs

Gutters

Seamless Gutters.
Zero Leaks.

Damaged or clogged gutters lead to foundation damage, fascia rot, and basement flooding — problems far more expensive than a gutter replacement. After a storm, most insurance policies cover gutter damage alongside roofing. We inspect and document it all in a single visit.

  • Seamless aluminum gutter installation
  • Storm damage insurance claims
  • Gutter guard & leaf protection systems
  • Gutter cleaning & maintenance
  • Downspout replacement & rerouting
  • Fascia & soffit repair

Solar

Cut Your Bills.
Own Your Power.

Storm damage often goes unnoticed on solar panels — micro-cracks, delamination, and mounting damage can slash your system's output without a visible sign. Forge Restoration inspects every panel, documents insurance-eligible damage, and installs high-efficiency systems that pay for themselves. Most homeowners qualify for federal tax credits on top of any insurance payout.

  • Hail & storm damage inspection for existing panels
  • Insurance claim documentation & advocacy
  • High-efficiency panel installation (monocrystalline)
  • Federal ITC tax credit guidance (up to 30%)
  • Battery storage & backup systems
  • Net metering setup & utility coordination

How It Works

The Insurance Claim
Process Made Easy

Most homeowners don't know they're entitled to a full replacement. Here's how we make it happen — at no stress to you.

1

Free Storm Inspection

We inspect your roof, siding, gutters, and windows for all storm damage — including damage homeowners can't see from the ground.

2

We File Your Claim

We help you document and file your insurance claim correctly the first time, with photos and a full damage report that leaves nothing on the table.

3

Adjuster Meeting

We attend the insurance adjuster walk-through with you and advocate for full replacement — not just a patch — backed by evidence.

4

Approval & Scheduling

Once approved, we order premium materials and schedule installation within days, not weeks. No limbo, no waiting.

5

Restoration Complete

New roof installed, final inspection passed, warranty issued. We handle all paperwork with your insurer so you can move on.

About Us

A Roof Over
Every Family's Head

With over 20 years of combined experience in solar energy and insurance claims, Forge Restoration was built on one idea — homeowners deserve someone in their corner. Co-founders BJ Drew and Jeremy McMillan have spent their careers on opposite sides of the same problem: BJ leading solar projects that save families thousands, and Jeremy working 12 years as a catastrophe insurance adjuster, seeing firsthand how overwhelming the claims process can be.

They started Forge Restoration to bring both worlds together — so you get a team that knows how to protect your home, fight for your claim, and find the smartest way to cut your energy costs.
